Domestic cleaning

Our domestic cleaning service keeps your home clean and safe when doing it yourself is hard because of disability, illness or age.

Make an enquiry

What it is

Our team can vacuum and mop, clean the kitchen and bathroom, do the washing, change the bed linen and take out the bins.

We follow the same checklist each visit, so you know what will be done. If something else needs attention, just tell us.

Espoir Care is a registered NDIS provider for household tasks, under registration group 0120.

Who it is for

  • NDIS participants with household tasks in their plan
  • Veterans approved for domestic assistance through Veterans' Home Care
  • People paying privately who need reliable help around the house

What a typical clean looks like

  1. We agree the jobs with you and how often you would like us to come.
  2. Your cleaner arrives at the booked time and checks if there is anything extra today.
  3. They work through the checklist, room by room.
  4. Before they leave, they check that you are happy with everything.

How it is funded

NDIS
Household tasks like cleaning can be funded from the core supports in your NDIS plan when you need help because of your disability.
DVA
Veterans can get domestic assistance through Veterans' Home Care after an assessment. Espoir Care is a Veterans' Home Care provider.
Private
You can pay for this service yourself. Our private rates follow the NDIS Pricing Arrangements, the same as for NDIS participants. We confirm the cost with you before we start.

Not sure how your support is funded? Ask us and we will help you work it out.

Questions people ask

How often can you come?

Weekly, fortnightly or on another schedule that suits you and your funding.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. We can talk with you about how we get in safely when you are out.

Is it the same cleaner each time?

We aim to send the same person or a small team, so they get to know you and your home.
