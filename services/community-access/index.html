Community access and participation

Community access means support to get out of the house and take part in the things you enjoy. It helps you build friendships, confidence and new skills.

Make an enquiry

What it is

A support worker goes with you to activities you choose. That could be shopping, the gym, a library group, church, a class, the footy, the beach or a cafe with friends.

We can also help you build the skills to go on your own one day, like planning the trip, catching public transport or paying at the counter.

Espoir Care is a registered NDIS provider for participation in community, social and civic activities, under registration group 0125.

Who it is for

  • NDIS participants with social and community participation in their plan
  • People who feel isolated or would like to meet new people
  • Young people building independence after leaving school

What a typical outing looks like

  1. You choose what you would like to do, or we help you come up with ideas.
  2. Your support worker meets you at home or at an agreed place.
  3. You head out together and they give as much or as little help as you want.
  4. Afterwards you talk about how it went and what you would like to do next time.

How it is funded

NDIS
Funded from the core supports in your NDIS plan, under help with social, economic and community participation. Entry fees and other activity costs are usually not covered by the NDIS. We will explain this before you start.
Private
You can pay for this service yourself. Our private rates follow the NDIS Pricing Arrangements, the same as for NDIS participants. We confirm the cost with you before we start.

Not sure how your support is funded? Ask us and we will help you work it out.

Questions people ask

Who pays for my ticket or my coffee?

You usually pay for your own activity costs. We will talk with you about any costs for your support worker before the outing.

Can my support worker drive me?

Yes, if transport is agreed in your plan and service agreement. Our travel and transport support page explains how it works.

Can I try something new?

Of course. Many people use this support to try a class, a sport or a volunteering role for the first time.

Related services

Travel and transport support

Help getting to appointments, work and outings. We can also help you learn to travel on your own.

Disability support services

Support workers who help with daily life, goals and getting out, planned around your NDIS goals.

Children's NDIS support

Support for young children and older kids at home and out and about, with breaks for parents.
