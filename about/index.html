About Espoir Care

We are a local disability support and nursing provider based in the Redlands, east of Brisbane. Until recently we were called Being Human Care.

Who we are

Espoir Care supports people with disability, veterans and families across Brisbane and the Redlands. Our support workers and nurses help with daily life, health care and getting out and about, in people's own homes and in the community.

We are a registered NDIS provider and a DVA contracted community nursing provider. We also provide services through Veterans' Home Care and to people who pay privately.

Our new name

We have changed our name from Being Human Care to Espoir Care. The business behind the name is the same, Being Human Care Service Pty Ltd, and our phone number and email address have not changed.

Why "espoir"?

Espoir is the French word for hope. When someone first calls us, they are often worried about a loved one or unsure where to turn. We want every visit to leave people feeling a bit more hopeful: that things can get easier, that they are not doing it alone and that life at home can keep going.

Our logo is a heart made of two people, side by side. That is how we see support: two people working it out together.

What we believe

Plain promises about how we work with you.

We listen first

You know your life best. We ask what matters to you before we suggest anything.

We respect your home

We are guests in your home and we act like it. Your routines and your way of doing things come first.

We are honest

If we cannot help, or something goes wrong, we tell you plainly and we work out what to do next together.

We keep things steady

We try to send the same familiar faces and keep to your routine, because trust takes time.

We work with your people

With your permission, we keep your family, coordinator and doctors in the loop, so nobody is left guessing.

Our team

Our support workers, nurses, cleaners and gardeners all live and work around Brisbane and the Redlands.
