About Espoir Care

We are a local disability support and nursing provider based in the Redlands, east of Brisbane. Until recently we were called Being Human Care.

Who we are

Espoir Care supports people with disability, veterans and families in Brisbane and the Redlands. Our support workers and nurses help with day to day living, health care and community access, at home or out in the community.

We are an NDIS registered provider and a community nursing provider contracted to DVA. We also provide Veterans' Home Care services and support clients who pay privately.

Espoir Care was founded by a registered nurse with 23 years of experience in hospitals and aged care facilities. Caring for people day to day in those places created a strong desire to support people and give them proper care.

Espoir Care brings that nursing experience into the home. Our nurses and support workers provide the same hands-on, attentive care, so people can stay in the home they know and families can feel reassured about their loved ones.

Our new name

We have rebranded and changed our name from Being Human Care to Espoir Care. We are still the same business, Being Human Care Service Pty Ltd, and our phone number and email address have not changed.

Why "espoir"?

Espoir is the French word for hope. When people first call us, they are often worried about a loved one or not sure what to do. Our goal is for people to feel a bit more hopeful after every visit. Hopeful that things can get easier, hopeful that they are not alone and hopeful that life at home can carry on.

Our logo is a heart made of two people side by side. This is how we see support: two people working on it together.

What we believe

Plain promises about how we work with you.

We listen first

You know your life best. We ask what matters to you before we suggest anything.

We respect your home

We are guests in your home and we act like it. Your routines and your way of doing things come first.

We are honest

If we cannot help, or something goes wrong, we tell you plainly and we work out what to do next together.

We keep things steady

We try to send the same familiar faces and keep to your routine, because trust takes time.

We work with your people

With your permission, we keep your family, coordinator and doctors in the loop, so nobody is left guessing.
