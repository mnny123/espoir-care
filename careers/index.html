Work with us

We would like to hear from kind, reliable people who want to help others live well at home.

Our vision

We want everyone we support to live well at home, with care they trust from people who know them. Espoir means hope, and we want every visit to leave people feeling a bit more hopeful.

We also want Espoir Care to be a place where good carers want to stay. That means fair pay, proper training, a team that backs you up and, where we can, the same clients week to week.

Working at Espoir Care

You will work one to one with clients in their homes and out in the community, across Brisbane and the Redlands. We try to keep the same workers with the same clients, so you get to know the people you support.

We pay in line with Fair Work guidelines and the award that covers your role.

You start with training on your induction day, then join monthly team meetings and ongoing training, so you keep learning and always have support.

Roles we hire

Support workers

Help people with personal care, daily tasks and getting out in the community.

Enrolled nurses

Give nursing care at home under the direction of a registered nurse.

Registered nurses

Assess, plan and give nursing care at home, including DVA community nursing.

Cleaners

Keep clients' homes clean and safe on a regular schedule.

Gardeners

Mow, weed and keep yards and paths safe and tidy.

What we look for

  • Kindness and patience
  • Turning up on time and doing what you said you would
  • Respect for people's privacy, culture and choices
  • Clear, friendly communication with clients and families
  • Willingness to learn and ask for help

Support workers need at least a Certificate III, or lived experience, in line with NDIS guidelines.

Checks you will need

  • NDIS Worker Screening Check
  • Blue Card (working with children check)
  • Current first aid and CPR certificates
  • A driver's licence and a reliable car, for some roles
  • AHPRA registration, for enrolled and registered nurses
