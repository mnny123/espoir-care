Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this website.

Our goal

We aim for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we have done

  • Every page can be used with a keyboard, with a clear focus outline.
  • Pages have a "skip to content" link, clear headings and landmarks for screen readers.
  • All text and background colours have been checked for contrast.
  • Every form field has a visible label, and errors are explained in words, not just colour.
  • The site works with JavaScript turned off. Without it, the enquiry and contact page sections show one after another instead of as tabs.
  • Animation is kept to small hover effects, and these switch off if you have asked your device to reduce motion.
  • Text can be resized, and the site works on small screens without scrolling sideways.
  • Each time the site is updated, it is tested with automated accessibility tools (axe and HTML_CodeSniffer, through Pa11y).
