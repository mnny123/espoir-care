How we can help

From a few hours of help each week to nursing care and support around the clock. Choose a service to find out who it is for and how it is funded.

Supported Independent Living (SIL)

Day to day support in a shared home, so you can live as independently as you can.

Short term accommodation (STA) and respite

Short stays with support, so you get a change of scene and your carers get a rest.

Community nursing

Nurses who visit you at home for wound care, medication support and other clinical care.

DVA community nursing

Nursing and personal care at home for veterans with a DVA Gold or White Card.

In-home nursing

Nursing at home for dementia, palliative care and getting back on your feet after hospital.

Personal care

Respectful help with showering, dressing, grooming and other personal tasks.

Disability support services

Support workers who help with daily life, goals and getting out, planned around your NDIS goals.

Home care for adults with disability

Regular help in your own home, so you can keep living the way you choose.

Assistance with personal activities

Help with meals and daily tasks, and overnight support so someone is close by at night.

Domestic cleaning

Regular cleaning so your home stays safe, clean and comfortable.

Gardening

Mowing, weeding and keeping paths clear, so your outdoor space is safe to use.

Community access and participation

Support to get out and about, see friends, join groups and try new things.

Travel and transport support

Help getting to appointments, work and outings. We can also help you learn to travel on your own.

Children's NDIS support

Support for young children and older kids at home and out and about, with breaks for parents.

Respite for carers

A break for family carers while we look after the person you care for.

About Espoir Care

Common questions

Do I need to be with the NDIS to use Espoir Care?

No. We support NDIS participants, veterans through DVA and private pay clients.

How much does it cost?

For NDIS participants, we charge according to the NDIS Pricing Arrangements. Approved DVA community nursing is paid for by DVA. For private pay clients, we give you a quote before we start.

Which areas do you cover?

We visit homes in Brisbane north, south and west and the Redlands, including Capalaba and Redland Bay. We also cover some nearby areas, so if you are not sure, call us with your suburb.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

We will respond within 1 to 2 business days. We will talk about what you need and answer your questions. If we are a good fit, we will set up a time to meet and plan your support together.

Did you used to be Being Human Care?

Yes. We changed our name from Being Human Care to Espoir Care. We are the same business, with the same phone number and email address.

Ready to talk?

Send us an enquiry or give us a call. We will listen, answer your questions and explain your options.

We reply to every enquiry within 1 to 2 business days.
